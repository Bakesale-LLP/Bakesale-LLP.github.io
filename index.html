Google Drive backup

Your data is safe, even if the machine isn't

The optional Drive backup copies the hourly server backup to a Google Drive account you choose. It only ever touches the one file it creates.

Manage Backups screen

Connect once

An admin clicks Connect Google Drive and approves access on Google's own sign-in page.

Hourly upload

Every hour the server saves a backup and replaces the previous copy in a "Bakesale Backup" folder.

You stay in control

Disconnect any time, or revoke access from your Google Account. See the Privacy Policy.

Privacy first

Bakesale POS requests only the drive.file permission. It can see and manage the single backup file it creates, and nothing else in your Drive. It does not read your emails, contacts or other files, and it never sells or shares your data.
